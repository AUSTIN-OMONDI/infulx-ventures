How it works

Every product is marked Small, Medium or Bulky (set it when you add or edit a product). Each area below has a price for each size. In the cart the customer picks their area and sees the exact delivery cost before paying. The biggest item pays the full rate and every extra item pays a share of its own rate, so buying more does not multiply the delivery. Leave a rate at 0 when you cannot quote it online – the customer is told you will confirm on WhatsApp.
