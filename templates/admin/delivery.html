How it works

In the cart the customer chooses their area from this list and that area's price is added to their total straight away – one delivery charge per order, whatever they buy. Leave a price at 0 for an area you cannot quote online: the customer is told you will send them the cost on WhatsApp before they pay. These prices come from the courier price list with KSh 100 added to every area.
